Planning to study medicine abroad requires careful financial foresight. This guide explains the cost categories Indian students should compare when considering FEFU and other Russian medical universities. The estimates below are public reference figures, not official fee quotes; confirm current amounts, currencies and payment terms directly with each university before making a budget.

Tuition and accommodation vary by university, program, cohort and room type. The table is a starting point for questions, not an NMC approval list or a recommendation; request each institution’s current fee schedule and contract.

Understanding the Elements of the Cost Structure

The total fee you pay consists of several distinct components. For non-FEFU rows in the table above, the RUB figures are approximate currency conversions from current public fee summaries, so always verify the latest university contract before counseling families.

  1. Tuition Fees: Paid directly to the university on a yearly or semester basis. Universities like FEFU support direct electronic transfers, preventing agents from adding hidden margins. Learn more about university criteria in the FEFU Admissions Guide.
  2. Dormitory & Accommodation: Varies by city. Older corridor hostels cost less, while FEFU's campus accommodation is officially quoted around 3,800 to 4,400 RUB per month. Check details in the FEFU Hostel Life Review.
  3. Health Insurance & Visas: Mandatory by Russian law. At FEFU, the School of Medicine Q&A mentions about 5,000 RUB yearly insurance, about 2,100 RUB yearly visa fees, and an annual medical checkup of about 8,000 RUB for medical students.
  4. Living Expenses: Food, internet, and pocket money can easily add another 15,000 to 25,000 RUB per month (about INR 0.20 to 0.33 lakh), depending on city and student routine.

A major risk for parents is the currency exchange fluctuation. Since tuition is finally paid against a university contract in Russian Rubles, the INR equivalent will move with the live rate. Working with direct university partners who sign a transparent direct-admission contract protects you from arbitrary agent surcharges.

FEFU Fee Reality Check for Indian Families

For FEFU, the latest official public fee references list 495,000 RUB per year for General Medicine in English. Older guidance summarized hostel cost around 44,000 RUB per year, while the School of Medicine Q&A gives a monthly hostel range of 3,800 to 4,400 RUB depending on room type. These numbers can change, so the final source must be the university contract.

A realistic budget should include more than tuition. FEFU's Q&A mentions medical insurance, annual visa extension, annual medical checkup for medical students, food, and winter clothing. That gives parents a clearer picture than a single "total package" number that hides daily costs.

The human way to present fees is simple: show tuition, hostel, food, insurance, visa, medical checkup, travel, and emergency buffer separately. Students should avoid any agency that refuses to show direct university payment instructions or mixes official tuition with service fees.

  • Use RUB first: quote official tuition in RUB, then show INR only as an approximate conversion.
  • Separate living costs: food and mess are not the same as hostel accommodation.
  • Protect trust: say "estimated budget" instead of "estimated total" unless every line is contract-backed.
