Is FEFU General Medicine Worth It? A Checklist for Indian Students

Updated October 12, 2026 5 min read

Updated October 12, 2026. This guide uses public university and regulator sources. Confirm details for your own intake before making a decision or payment.

In India, students often use “MBBS” to describe undergraduate medicine abroad. FEFU calls its program General Medicine. Whether it is a sensible choice depends on the current offer, your study and language plans, the complete cost, and the rules that apply if you want to practise in India.

Start with the current course documents

FEFU’s published 2024 General Medicine curriculum lists a full-time, six-year English-language program. That document helps explain the published plan; it does not establish availability, fees, seat counts, or terms for a later intake. Check the FEFU admissions portal for current instructions and ask the university to confirm any point that affects your application.

Use a fit checklist, not a sales pitch

  • Course: Can you review the current program description, curriculum, teaching language, and year-by-year structure?
  • Campus: FEFU’s official student information places the university at 10 Ajax Bay on Russky Island, Vladivostok. Consider whether that setting and travel distance suit you.
  • Language and clinical learning: Ask FEFU when and how language learning and patient-facing training are organized for your cohort. Do not assume an English-medium course means all clinical communication is in English.
  • Accommodation: Get current details of the residence, room type, availability, rules, included services, and charges directly from FEFU.
  • Full budget: Request the current university fee schedule and list living, travel, insurance, visa-related, and other third-party costs separately. Do not rely on old currency conversions or copied hostel prices.

Check the India return path before enrolment

The National Medical Commission’s official rules page lists the Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations 2021 and related FAQs, including FAQs dated June 8, 2026. Read the current rules with your family and confirm how the requirements apply to your course and cohort. A university or consultancy cannot promise licensing eligibility, a pass in any examination, or registration in India.

Ask for written answers before paying

  1. Which intake and exact General Medicine program are covered by the offer?
  2. What are the current admission documents, deadlines, fees, and official payment instructions?
  3. What residence and other university charges should your family budget for?
  4. Which visa documents does FEFU provide, and which documents must you submit through the official authority?
  5. If you use a consultancy, what service is covered by the signed agreement, what is the single consultancy fee, and which university or third-party costs are separate?
