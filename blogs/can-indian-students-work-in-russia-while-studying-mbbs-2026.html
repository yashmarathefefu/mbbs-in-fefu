Can Indian Students Work in Russia While Studying MBBS? 2026

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 6 min read
Rules and opportunities can change: This is general information. Confirm work eligibility with official Russian authorities and FEFU before accepting a job.

“Can I work part-time while studying MBBS in Russia?” is a sensible question, especially when families are building a six-year budget. The official Study in Russia portal says full-time international students at state-accredited universities may work in their free time without a separate work permit, subject to the stated conditions. Confirm that the rule applies to your status and documents.

Financial planning answer: Treat work as optional experience, not as a source you need to pay tuition or rent. Jobs, hours and earnings are not guaranteed, and medical study requires consistent time.

Do Not Build Your Tuition Plan Around a Future Job

A student arriving in Vladivostok may need time to settle in, learn daily Russian and understand the academic routine. A suitable vacancy may not be available when you need one. A medical course can also have a demanding timetable. Plan core expenses from confirmed funds, not estimated part-time earnings.

If you are interested in working, ask FEFU about current career support and student opportunities. Confirm whether the job is on or off campus, what documents the employer needs, the permitted schedule and how the role fits your visa conditions. Do not rely on old forum advice.
