A deadline question sounds simple: “What is the last date?” In international admission, that is rarely the safest question. A file submitted on the final date can still be too late for entrance testing, invitation processing, visa stamping and an orderly arrival.

What you should do now: if you are targeting the 2026 FEFU intake, prepare and verify your file immediately. Treat any month-by-month schedule below as a planning calendar, not an official deadline notice. Ask for the current General Medicine deadline and the last safe date for invitation/visa processing in writing.

Why “Apply Early” Is More Than Marketing

Document correction takes real time

A passport renewal, spelling affidavit or fresh scan can cost days or weeks. Early review gives you room to fix it without panic.

Invitation and visa are separate stages

An admission-related document does not automatically mean a student can travel. The invitation and visa process has its own checks and timing.

FEFU is in Vladivostok, not Moscow

Travel needs to be planned for the actual final destination. A student should understand the itinerary, baggage transfer and campus arrival support before booking.

Red flag: anyone promising “guaranteed admission in 24 hours” without reviewing eligibility, program, documents and payment destination is removing checks you actually need.

Four Questions to Ask Before Calling Your FEFU File Complete

  1. What exact program and intake year are shown on my paperwork?
  2. Which remaining step depends on the university, invitation authority, embassy or me?
  3. What is the last safe date—not merely the last advertised date?
  4. Who receives each payment and which document explains it?
