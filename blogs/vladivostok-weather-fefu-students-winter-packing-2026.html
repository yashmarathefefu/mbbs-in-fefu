Vladivostok Weather for FEFU Students: Winter Packing and Health Guide

Student Arrival Team June 28, 2026 8 min read

Students from India often imagine Russian winter as a contest to find the thickest jacket. In Vladivostok, wind, changing indoor/outdoor temperatures and slippery ground matter just as much as the number on a weather app.

The practical rule: pack a layering system, not a suitcase full of random woollens. Carry enough protection for arrival, check the current forecast, and buy specialist deep-winter items locally if the fit, quality and budget are better.

The Six-Piece Winter System

  1. Base layer: thermal top and bottom that move moisture away from skin.
  2. Mid-layer: fleece or wool for insulation.
  3. Outer layer: insulated and wind-resistant jacket suitable for local conditions.
  4. Feet: warm socks and shoes with reliable grip.
  5. Hands/head/neck: gloves, hat and neck protection—the pieces students often forget.
  6. Indoor transition: clothing that can be removed easily inside heated buildings.

What to Pack from India—and What Can Wait

Bring prescriptions, a doctor’s letter where needed, essential regular medicines, glasses/contact-lens supplies, a basic personal-care kit and enough cold protection to arrive safely. Check airline and customs rules for medicines and food.

Do not sacrifice documents, a reliable laptop or luggage weight for multiple cheap bulky jackets. One well-planned set plus a local purchase is often more useful.

Medical note: this is general preparation advice, not personal medical guidance. Students with asthma, severe allergies, skin disease or other conditions should discuss cold-weather plans and medications with their clinician before travel.
