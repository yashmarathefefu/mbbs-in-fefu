FEFU MBBS Admission Process 2026 for Indian Students

MBBS Abroads Team May 23, 2026 8 min read

Short answer: the FEFU MBBS admission process for Indian students usually starts with eligibility and document review, then university application, Biology and Chemistry entrance exams, admission contract or offer steps, visa support, travel planning, and hostel arrival in Vladivostok.

If your target is MBBS in FEFU, do not treat it like a generic Russia application. FEFU has its own School of Medicine process, entrance subjects, deadlines, hostel system, and arrival routine.

Far Eastern Federal University is one of the more serious choices for Indian students who want a campus-based medical route in Russia. The official FEFU School of Medicine education page lists General Medicine (31.05.01) as a 6-year program with an English-medium option and Biology and Chemistry entrance exams. That makes the admission process clearer than many vague “MBBS abroad” offers.

Source check: FEFU's School of Medicine admission page mentions application requests, an application fee, Biology and Chemistry entrance exams, online English exams, and a document deadline for international applicants needing visa support. Students must still verify the current intake instructions and final fee contract before payment.

Step 1: Check Whether FEFU Fits Your Goal

Before starting documents, decide whether FEFU is the right environment. FEFU is best for students who want a structured 6-year General Medicine program, a real campus, shared hostel life, and a quieter academic routine in Vladivostok. It is not only a fee decision.

Read this with our Why Choose FEFU for MBBS in Russia guide if you are still comparing whether FEFU is the right university for your family.

Step 2: Prepare Academic and Identity Documents

Indian students should prepare documents before the intake rush starts. At minimum, keep your passport, Class 10 documents, Class 12 marksheet, NEET result, passport-size photos, and translated or notarized copies ready where required. If a document is not in English or Russian, expect translation requirements.

  • Passport with enough validity for admission and visa processing.
  • Class 12 marksheet with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology details.
  • NEET qualification proof for Indian licensing planning.
  • Medical, HIV, and other visa-related documents when requested.
  • Official university forms, contract, and payment receipt after approval.

Step 3: Apply to FEFU and Prepare for Entrance Exams

FEFU's School of Medicine admission information says General Medicine entrance exams are Biology and Chemistry. The page describes the exams as online and in English, with different question formats. This is important because students should prepare for subject tests, not only submit documents and wait.

For Indian students, Biology and Chemistry are familiar because of NEET preparation, but FEFU's test pattern is still separate. Keep enough time for university instructions, login details, test dates, and retest rules if any.

Step 4: Understand Offer, Contract, and Fee Payment

Do not mix official FEFU tuition with agent service charges. The safest process is to separate official university fees, hostel costs, insurance, visa, medical checkup, travel, and any counselling/service support. For fee planning, use our FEFU MBBS fees and hostel guide.

Older and newer public fee references can differ by academic year, so the final source should be the current FEFU contract or invoice. Always keep receipts and written communication.

Step 5: Plan Visa, Hostel, and Vladivostok Arrival

Admission is not complete when a student gets an offer. The family still needs visa support, travel planning, airport coordination, and hostel settlement. For parents, this is where FEFU's campus model becomes valuable because student life is easier to understand visually and practically.

Review the FEFU hostel and campus life guide and the FEFU campus gallery before finalizing the decision.

Step 6: Keep NMC/FMGL Documentation Safe From Day One

Indian students must think beyond admission. Keep language-of-instruction proof, course duration documents, admission letter, fee receipts, visa copies, and academic records. These documents may matter later for India-return licensing and verification.

Use our NMC guidelines checklist for MBBS abroad before paying any major amount. A serious FEFU admission process should make every document clearer, not more confusing.

Final Takeaway

The best FEFU MBBS admission process is not the fastest one. It is the one where the student knows the program, entrance subjects, documents, fees, hostel, visa steps, and NMC/FMGL checks before paying. If the process feels rushed or unclear, slow down and verify.

For Indian students targeting MBBS in FEFU in 2026, the strongest strategy is simple: prepare documents early, verify every official step, and choose FEFU because the campus, program, and support system fit your long-term medical goal.
