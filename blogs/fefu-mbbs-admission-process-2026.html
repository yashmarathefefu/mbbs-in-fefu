FEFU General Medicine Admission Paperwork Checklist for Indian Students

Updated October 13, 2026 5 min read
Independent consultancy guide. This is not an official FEFU or National Medical Commission page. Intake details below should be checked with the source university or regulator.

Updated October 13, 2026. This checklist is for Indian applicants considering General Medicine at Far Eastern Federal University (FEFU), often called MBBS in India.

Admission instructions can change by intake. Use this guide to organize questions and documents; use FEFU’s current portal for the rules that apply to your application.

1. Confirm the course and intake

Start with FEFU’s official admissions portal. Confirm the exact program name, study language, academic year, available places, deadlines, current fees, and how applicants in your category should apply. FEFU’s linked 2024 General Medicine curriculum is a reference for that published plan, not proof of later intake availability or terms.

2. Ask for the current document checklist

Request FEFU’s list for your program, intake, and applicant category. Keep identity and passport documents, academic records, exam or eligibility documents, and translations organized, but do not assume every item on an old checklist is required or that a current requirement is absent because another page omits it.

  • Ask which documents must be submitted and which are needed only later.
  • Confirm accepted file formats, translations, certifications, and submission route.
  • Check how FEFU wants names, dates, and passport details entered.
  • Keep a copy of each version you submit and the university’s response.

3. Confirm entrance tests directly with FEFU

If the current instructions require entrance tests, confirm the subjects, exam format, language, dates, login method, and any retake policy with FEFU. Do not treat a previous year’s subject list or format as a current rule.

4. Read the offer and fee terms before paying

Compare the offer and contract with the program and intake you applied for. Ask FEFU to confirm the current fee schedule, currency, payment recipient, deadlines, and refund terms in writing. Keep university tuition, residence charges, and other third-party costs separate from any consultancy service fee.

If you work with a consultancy, read a signed agreement that states the service and one disclosed consultancy fee. Ask for any university or third-party expenses to be identified separately before you pay.

5. Organize visa documents through official instructions

After FEFU explains the next steps for your application, confirm which university documents it will issue and which documents you must prepare or submit through the relevant official visa channel. Follow that authority’s current checklist. FEFU makes admission decisions; the relevant authorities decide visa applications and processing times.

6. Confirm accommodation and the complete budget

Ask FEFU for the residence assignment process, current availability, room type, rules, included services, charges, and payment terms for your intake. Separately budget for travel, food, insurance, visa-related costs, and personal expenses using current quotes. Do not rely on undated hostel prices or currency conversions copied from older pages.

7. Check requirements for your plans in India

The National Medical Commission’s rules and regulations page lists the Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations 2021 and related FAQs, including an update dated 8 June 2026. Read the current material with your family and verify how it applies to your course and cohort before enrolment. No university or consultancy can guarantee an exam result or registration outcome.
