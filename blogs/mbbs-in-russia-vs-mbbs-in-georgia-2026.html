MBBS in Russia vs MBBS in Georgia: Complete Comparative Guide

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team March 16, 2026 8 min read

For Indian medical candidates looking abroad, Eastern Europe offers excellent opportunities. Russia and Georgia are the top two destinations. However, choosing between them requires understanding their distinct educational and financial frameworks. In this guide, we provide a side-by-side comparison of MBBS in Russia vs Georgia in 2026.

1. Financial Comparison (Subsidized vs Private)

The biggest differentiator is the cost structure. Russian public universities are subsidized, but families should stop relying on older "under 30 lakhs" Russia brochures because exchange rates and newer university fee tables can materially change the final INR budget. Georgian universities are often private and usually land at a higher overall cost once tuition and living expenses are converted into INR.

2. Course Duration and Internship Laws

Both countries comply with the latest NMC FMGL Regulations. The program duration is 6 years, which includes 5 years of academic instruction and 1 year of clinical internship within the same country. However, Russia's clinical exposure is often higher due to the massive capacity of public hospitals compared to private Georgian clinics.

3. Student Accommodation and Comfort

While students in Georgia often have to rent private apartments in cities like Tbilisi, Russian federal universities like FEFU provide structured campus housing. The island dorms at FEFU feature attached bathrooms and secure access. Read more details in the FEFU Campus Life Review.

FEFU-Specific Decision Check: Russia vs Georgia

Most Russia-vs-Georgia articles online sound the same because they compare countries in broad slogans. The better comparison for Indian students is program-level: Does this specific university meet the student's NMC/FMGL checklist, budget, language needs, and clinical-training expectations?

FEFU gives you several concrete points to compare: General Medicine is listed as a 6-year English-medium program, entrance exams are Biology and Chemistry, the campus has on-campus hostels, and the School of Medicine notes access to FEFU Medical Center. Those are specific facts students can compare against any Georgian university offer letter.

Georgia may be attractive for some students because of location, climate, or European-facing positioning. But Indian students should compare the exact duration, internship route, local licensing eligibility, hospital exposure, and total budget. Do not choose a country from a YouTube ranking alone.

  • Ask both universities: Is the whole course offline and English-medium?
  • Ask both universities: How is internship or clinical clerkship documented?
  • Ask both universities: Is the degree registrable in the country where it is awarded?
