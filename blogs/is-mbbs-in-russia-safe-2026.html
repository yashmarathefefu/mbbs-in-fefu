There is no responsible yes-or-no answer that can guarantee a student's safety. Conditions, individual needs, travel plans, and university arrangements can change. Families considering FEFU should check current official guidance and get intake-specific campus, accommodation, health, and emergency details in writing before making plans.

A campus location, online review, or consultancy statement cannot replace current information from FEFU, the relevant authorities, and the student's insurer. This guide focuses on questions to verify rather than claiming that any country or university is risk-free.

Verify the arrangements that apply to your FEFU intake

  • Program and intake: Check FEFU's current English-medium admissions listing for the program and intake information, then ask the university about any item that is unclear or not yet published.
  • Accommodation: FEFU publishes double-room options and listed charges. Confirm your assigned building, occupancy, access and visitor rules, move-in steps, and current charge directly with FEFU.
  • Student support: Ask FEFU for the current student-support and accommodation contacts, their working hours and languages, and the route to use after hours. Do not assume the same contact or services apply to every building or intake.
  • Health and emergencies: Request the university's current medical and emergency instructions. Ask the insurer for the policy, emergency number, covered providers, exclusions, and how direct payment or reimbursement works.
  • Arrival and local travel: Recheck your full itinerary, transfer arrangements, arrival contact, and first-night accommodation close to departure. Routes, availability, and provider terms can change.

Use current guidance from India

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) provides guidance for students going abroad, including registration through MADAD and advice to understand local rules and living conditions. Its foreign-travel guidance recommends checking the latest travel advisories and arranging appropriate travel and medical insurance. The MEA's student FAQ links country-specific student advisories, including one for the Russian Federation.

Make a contact plan before departure

Save and carry the contacts FEFU and your insurer confirm for your case. Include the relevant university student and accommodation offices, the after-hours or emergency pathway, your insurer's assistance line, a family contact in India, and the contact details for India's mission that you verify from an official government page. Keep essential admission, visa, insurance, and accommodation documents accessible both on paper and offline.
