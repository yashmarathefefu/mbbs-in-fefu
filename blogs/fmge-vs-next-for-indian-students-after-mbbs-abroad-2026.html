FMGE vs NExT After MBBS Abroad: What Indian Students Need to Know

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 7 min read
Time-sensitive regulatory topic: This article reports what official NMC material said in 2026. It is not a prediction of which examination will apply to a student graduating years from now.

Indian students planning an overseas medical degree often hear both “FMGE” and “NExT.” The names can sound like two exams a student must take, but the question is really about which licensing framework is operational when a graduate applies for registration.

As of the NMC’s March 2026 FAQ: NExT was not operational at that time. The FAQ said that, until implementation, the existing registration system would continue. Check the latest NMC and NBEMS notices before acting; exam policy can change.

What the 2026 NMC Notices Mean for Applicants

The NMC’s March 2026 FAQ described NExT as not yet operational and explained that, upon implementation, it is intended to serve as a common qualifying and licentiate examination. This is a dated snapshot, not a permanent promise. The National Medical Commission can issue further notifications, and the requirements in force at graduation may differ from those in force at enrolment.

For a student starting a six-year course, a responsible plan is to follow current rules for admission and preserve course documents throughout study. Keep curricula, attendance, clinical rotation schedules, internship records and official transcripts. A licensing exam is only one part of the eligibility pathway.

Our NMC guide for overseas medical study covers the broader pre-admission checks, while the FMGE results guide explains why past exam statistics are not a personal guarantee.

How to Plan for FEFU and an India Return

  1. Check the current NMC rules before admission, especially course length, language and training.
  2. Confirm FEFU’s batch-specific General Medicine curriculum and the documents it issues.
  3. Maintain a personal archive of university records each year.
  4. Near graduation, check current NMC and NBEMS notices rather than relying on old coaching material.
  5. Prepare for the examination framework actually announced for your cohort.
