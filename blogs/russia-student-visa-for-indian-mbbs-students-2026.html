Russia Student Visa for Indian MBBS Students: FEFU Guide 2026

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 7 min read
Visa rules change: This checklist is general information, not an immigration decision. Follow the latest instructions from FEFU and the Russian consular office responsible for your application.

For a student headed to Vladivostok, the visa process begins after the university confirms the admission steps. The key is to keep the sequence clear: official admission, university invitation, consular application, then travel. A consultant or travel agent cannot replace a visa decision by the Russian authorities.

Plan around the study purpose. A Russian e-visa and a student visa are not interchangeable just because both permit entry. Ask FEFU which visa category fits your course and intake before booking flights.

Questions to Confirm Before You Apply

Ask whether the invitation is issued electronically or in paper form, which passport-validity period applies, what health documents are needed, whether translations must be notarized, and where applicants in your consular jurisdiction submit the file. The Russian consular website lists visa categories and may update forms or requirements; use that current list rather than a forwarded checklist from a previous year.

Keep copies of the admission letter, invitation, visa application, insurance information and travel details in a secure place. Once in Russia, follow the university’s instructions for arrival notification and migration registration promptly. An approved visa does not remove post-arrival obligations.

Do Not Book Travel Around an Assumption

Wait for the documents and visa timing advised by FEFU and the consular office. Ticket prices can change, but buying a non-refundable ticket before the visa is issued can create avoidable risk. Do not overstay or work outside the conditions attached to your immigration status.
