How to Reach FEFU from India: Flight, Airport and Arrival Guide 2026

Student Arrival Team June 28, 2026 8 min read

The cheapest ticket is not always the cheapest journey. A low fare can hide self-transfer, baggage re-check, airport changes, difficult transit rules or an overnight connection that a first-time traveler is not prepared for.

Destination: plan the itinerary to Vladivostok International Airport, then continue to FEFU’s Russky Island campus using the confirmed arrival arrangement. Routes from India change, so verify every transit country, baggage rule and airport before payment.

How to Compare Flight Options

  • One booking reference: usually simpler when delays affect a connection.
  • Baggage: confirm whether it goes through to Vladivostok.
  • Transit visa: verify rules for your nationality, route and airport.
  • Connection time: leave enough margin for security and terminal movement.
  • Arrival time: coordinate with the actual pickup or transport window.

Your Cabin-Baggage Document File

Carry passport, visa, invitation, university admission/contract papers, fee receipts, insurance, accommodation and emergency contacts. Add prescriptions and essential medicine, a charged phone, power bank within airline rules, some local-accessible funds and a change of clothes.

Keep the FEFU campus address offline: 10 Ajax Bay, Russky Island, Vladivostok. Also keep the current responsible contact, not only a group-chat name.

The First 24 Hours Matter

FEFU’s published foreign-student residence rules require prompt handling of passport, visa and migration-card registration after arrival. Do not keep the migration card loose in a pocket. Photograph it, store the original safely and follow current university instructions.

Never hand your passport to an unverified person. Use official university offices or clearly identified, authorized support and obtain a receipt/acknowledgement where appropriate.
