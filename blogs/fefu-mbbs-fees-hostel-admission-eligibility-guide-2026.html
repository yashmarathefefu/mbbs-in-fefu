FEFU MBBS Fees, Hostel, Admission and Eligibility Guide (2026 Intake)

Ed-Counselor Team March 14, 2026 8 min read

Choosing the right institution is the first step toward a successful medical career. Far Eastern Federal University (FEFU) in Vladivostok stands out as one of Russia's top-ranked public universities, offering a fully English-medium MBBS program tailored to meet global standards. This comprehensive guide covers everything from eligibility requirements to fee schedules for 2026.

1. Direct Academic Eligibility Criteria

Indian students must satisfy the following criteria to be eligible for FEFU MBBS admissions:

  • Age Requirement: The candidate must be at least 17 years old on or before December 31st of the admission year.
  • Academic Marks: Must have passed Class 12 with a minimum score of 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) for General category candidates, and 40% for SC/ST/OBC.
  • NEET Requirement: A qualifying score in the NEET-UG examination of the current year (or the preceding two years) is mandatory, complying with the NMC FMGL Regulations.

3. Fees and Hostel Overview

FEFU offers a modern campus-living setup. The newer public fee references list General Medicine at 495,000 RUB per year (about INR 6.63 lakh at the exchange basis used in this site), while hostel accommodation is usually quoted around 3,800 to 4,400 RUB per month (about INR 5.1k to 5.9k). You can read a wider budget breakdown in the MBBS in Russia Fees Guide.

2026 Fee and Admission Source Check

The most useful update for this guide is the official FEFU 2025/2026 admission table. It lists General Medicine in English at 495,000 RUB per year, with Biology and Chemistry as the two entrance tests and 250 places for foreign citizens. An older School of Medicine admission page listed 440,000 RUB for 2024/2025, so students should always follow the amount written in the latest university contract.

FEFU's School of Medicine admission page also states that entrance exams are in English, online, and include Chemistry and Biology. It mentions an application fee of 3,500 RUB and an August 8 document deadline for international applicants needing visa support. These details make the admission journey feel real because students can see exactly which step happens before enrollment.

For India practice eligibility, avoid treating any foreign university as automatically approved. Use the safer SEO phrase NMC/FMGL-compliant medical program and link it to document checks. Students should compare FEFU's program duration, English instruction, clinical training, internship route, and host-country registration eligibility against the latest NMC/FMGL rules.

  • Official fee check: verify tuition in RUB on FEFU's current admission page before payment.
  • Official program check: General Medicine (31.05.01), 6 years, English instruction, Biology and Chemistry entrance exams.
  • Official document check: passport, academic certificate, notarized translation, application account, contract, and payment receipt.
