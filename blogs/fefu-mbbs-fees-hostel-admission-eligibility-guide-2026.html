FEFU MBBS Fees and Hostel: 2026/27 Guide

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team Updated October 16, 2026 8 min read

This guide summarizes FEFU’s published 2026/27 English-medium General Medicine tuition and current double-room hostel charges for Indian families. FEFU’s official pages are the source for its current program list and accommodation schedule; confirm availability and personal eligibility with the university before paying.

For the step-by-step application route, open the FEFU MBBS admission process guide for Indian students and use this page as the budget companion.

1. 2026/27 Program and Eligibility Checks

FEFU’s current English-medium listing shows General Medicine (31.05.01), Biology and Chemistry as entrance subjects, 250 places for foreign citizens, and tuition of RUB 500,000 per year. That listing does not replace an applicant-specific eligibility decision.

  • Ask FEFU for the current application checklist and eligibility rules for your applicant category and intake.
  • Check the current NEET-UG information from NTA and the NMC rules and regulations relevant to your intended India licensing pathway.
  • Do not use an estimated NEET score, an old percentage cutoff, or a consultancy summary as a substitute for current official instructions.

2. Step-by-Step Admission Process

Use the dates and steps in FEFU’s current instructions for your intake. This sequence is a planning aid, not a published deadline calendar:

Phase Action Steps Timeline
Phase 1 Confirm the program, intake, application route, current eligibility instructions, and official document checklist. Use FEFU’s current intake dates
Phase 2 Prepare and submit the requested documents; confirm any entrance tests, format, language, and schedule with FEFU. Use FEFU’s current intake dates
Phase 3 Follow FEFU’s admission decision and coordinate any university invitation documents needed for the next step. Set by FEFU
Phase 4 Prepare visa documents, arrange travel, coordinate FEFU hostel move-in, and plan arrival and post-arrival paperwork. Authority and travel timelines vary

3. Fees and Hostel Overview

For 2026/27, FEFU lists General Medicine tuition at RUB 500,000 per year. Its accommodation page lists double-room charges per bed, per month: RUB 3,100–3,200 for 18–20 m² rooms in buildings 1.8 and 2.1–2.6; RUB 3,500–4,200 for 25–32 m² rooms in buildings 6–11, depending on room type and mini-fridge option. These university charges are separate from the consultancy fee. See FEFU’s 2026/27 English-medium admissions listing and official double-room rate schedule; rates checked 16 October 2026.

2026/27 Fee and Admission Source Check

Checked 16 October 2026: FEFU’s current English-medium admissions listing shows General Medicine in English at RUB 500,000 per year, with Biology and Chemistry as entrance subjects and 250 places for foreign citizens. Confirm that applications remain open and verify the current contract and payment schedule directly with FEFU.

The listed hostel rates are monthly, per bed, and depend on the room/building option and mini-fridge choice. FEFU may update both tuition and accommodation charges; do not rely on a previous intake’s fees, payment terms, or room availability.

For India practice eligibility, read the current NMC rules and regulations for your cohort and separately confirm the applicable NEET-UG information with NTA. University admission does not itself establish future licensing eligibility or exam outcomes.

  • Official fee check: verify tuition in RUB on FEFU's current admission page before payment.
  • Official program check: General Medicine (31.05.01), English-medium listing, entrance subjects, annual tuition, and places for the stated 2026/27 cycle.
  • Official document check: request FEFU’s current intake-specific list and confirm any translation or certification requirements before submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

FEFU’s 2026/2027 English-medium admissions listing shows Biology and Chemistry as entrance subjects for General Medicine. Confirm the current format, dates, and applicant-specific rules on FEFU’s admissions portal.

There is no fixed score to rely on here. Check the current NTA NEET-UG qualifying notice and the NMC rules that apply to your intended cohort and India licensing plan.
