NMC Guidelines for MBBS Abroad 2026: Mandatory Compliance Checklist

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team March 20, 2026 8 min read

For any Indian student looking at a medical degree abroad, compliance with the National Medical Commission (NMC) is essential. A non-compliant degree means you cannot practice medicine in India. In this guide, we break down the NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) Regulations for 2026, showing how Far Eastern Federal University (FEFU) complies with every rule.

If FEFU is your target, combine this checklist with the FEFU MBBS admission process guide so the admission steps and India-return documents stay aligned from day one.

The 4 Essential Pillars of NMC Compliance

According to the official NMC gazette, every student studying MBBS abroad must meet these four requirements:

  1. Minimum Course Duration: The academic program must span a minimum of 54 months (4.5 years) of medical instruction.
  2. Mandatory Clinical Internship: Students must complete a 12-month clinical internship at the same foreign university hospital.
  3. English Medium of Instruction: The entire course, including lectures, practicals, and examinations, must be taught in English. Bilingual courses do not qualify.
  4. License to Practice in Host Country: The medical degree must grant the graduate registration or a license to practice medicine in the host country (e.g., Russia).

How FEFU Complies with the Regulations

FEFU's medical program is structured to meet all NMC requirements:

  • Program Duration: The curriculum spans 6 years, exceeding the 54-month academic instruction requirement.
  • On-Campus Internship: The 6th year features a clinical internship at the advanced FEFU Medical Center.
  • 100% English Medium: The program is fully English-medium, which is documented on the student's transcripts. Learn more about the academic environment in the FEFU Honest Review.
  • Licensing Eligibility: FEFU graduates are eligible to take the Russian State Exam, granting them registration to practice locally, which satisfies the NMC licensing clause.

To qualify for admissions, students must also meet the minimum NEET score, as outlined in our FEFU Admission Eligibility Guide.

How FEFU Should Be Checked Against NMC/FMGL Rules

Students search for "NMC-approved medical colleges in Russia," but the official NMC student-abroad page says NMC does not endorse any list of foreign medical institutions. The safer and more accurate SEO phrase is NMC/FMGL-compliant medical program.

For FEFU, the useful source-backed checks are clear: the official School of Medicine page lists General Medicine as 6 years, English-medium, with Biology and Chemistry entrance exams; the admissions page gives application and entrance-test details; and the School Q&A explains hostel, security, and student-life infrastructure. These facts support a compliance review, but students must still verify the latest NMC/FMGL regulations before payment.
