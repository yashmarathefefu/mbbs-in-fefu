MBBS in Russia vs Private Medical College in India: Cost Guide

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 7 min read
Cost information varies: Fees change by intake and institution. This guide is a comparison framework, not a quote. Use current official fee documents for any decision.

Families comparing MBBS in Russia with a private medical college in India often start with tuition. That number matters, but it does not answer the full question. You need to compare the total cost over the complete course, using current written documents and the living arrangement that actually applies to your family.

Build a Six-Year Budget You Can Explain

Write down each expense, its source, the year it is due, the currency and the receiving organization. Include one-time costs such as flights, initial settlement and document work, but do not confuse a one-time university expense with a consultancy service fee. Ask whether tuition can change each year and what happens if a student withdraws or defers.

For FEFU, get the current General Medicine fee quote and hostel terms from the university. Then use an exchange-rate range rather than one conversion made today. For an Indian college, compare the relevant college’s official schedule and local living costs. A broad national “average” can hide the actual difference between two offers.

Compare More Than Price

The right choice also depends on language, distance from family, clinical training, academic support, the student’s ability to adapt and the licensing requirements in the country where they plan to practise. If the student wants to return to India, check the applicable NMC requirements before paying either institution.

For the Russian side of the estimate, use our MBBS in Russia fee breakdown and FEFU-specific fee guide as checklists, then confirm the current amounts directly with the university.
