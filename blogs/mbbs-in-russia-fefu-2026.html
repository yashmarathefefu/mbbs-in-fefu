FEFU General Medicine for Indian Students: 2026/27 Checklist

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team Updated October 22, 2026

This page is a FEFU-specific checklist for Indian students considering General Medicine. It uses FEFU’s current 2026/27 admissions listing and official accommodation schedule; ask the university to confirm current terms for your applicant category before you apply or pay.

FEFU General Medicine: 2026/27 listing

FEFU’s English-medium admissions page lists General Medicine (31.05.01), with Biology and Chemistry entrance subjects, 250 places, and tuition of RUB 500,000 per year for the 2026/27 intake. The admissions page and contract are the authority for current requirements, availability, payment timing, and final charges.

Open FEFU’s 2026/27 English-medium admissions listing.

Questions to resolve before applying

  1. Applicant eligibility: Ask FEFU which academic documents, entrance subjects, language evidence, and deadlines apply to your applicant category.
  2. Indian regulatory checks: Review the National Medical Commission’s current information on foreign medical qualifications and verify which rules apply to your circumstances. Check current NEET information through the National Testing Agency’s medical exam page.
  3. Program documents: Ask FEFU for the curriculum and contract that apply to your intake. You can also review the published General Medicine curriculum and confirm whether a newer version applies.
  4. Written cost details: Keep each amount in its stated currency and billing period. Ask who receives each payment and get current payment instructions from that provider.

Official FEFU double-room charges

Checked 23 October 2026, FEFU’s double-room page lists these charges per bed per month:

  • Buildings 1.8 and 2.1–2.6, 18–20 m²: RUB 3,100; RUB 3,200 with the mini-fridge option.
  • Buildings 6–11, 25–32 m²: RUB 3,500–4,100 depending on room type; RUB 3,600–4,200 with the mini-fridge option.

See FEFU’s official double-room descriptions and rate schedule. FEFU assigns accommodation and confirms the available room and current charge. These university charges are separate from tuition and the consultancy fee.
