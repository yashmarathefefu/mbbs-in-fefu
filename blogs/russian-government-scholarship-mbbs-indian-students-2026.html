Russian Government Scholarships for Indian MBBS Students: What to Verify

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 7 min read
Check each cycle: Scholarship availability, quotas, eligibility and dates can change. This is a verification checklist, not a current-cycle scholarship notice. Confirm the active call and its requirements through the official portal before making plans.

“Is MBBS in Russia free on scholarship?” deserves a careful answer. Government scholarship routes may be offered in specific cycles, but medicine, eligibility, language of study and university placement must be confirmed in the active official notice. A scholarship route does not mean every applicant or chosen medical program receives an award.

Start with current official instructions: Check the education-in-russia.com portal and any linked notice for the active cycle. Read the written award terms to see what is covered; budget for any living, travel, visa, insurance or other expenses not expressly included.

Do Not Confuse a Scholarship with “Everything Is Free”

Do not assume an award covers every personal cost. Ask for written confirmation about tuition, hostel, food, insurance, airfare, visa, translations, books and day-to-day expenses before accepting an award or comparing it with a self-funded offer.

Also confirm whether the English-track General Medicine program is available under the current quota and whether FEFU can be selected. A university preference is not the same as a confirmed scholarship placement. If the scholarship route is unsuccessful, compare the self-funded option using FEFU’s official fee documents rather than a generic Russia estimate.

Keep the Scholarship Application in Your Own Name

Use the official portal linked below and retain your login, submitted forms and confirmation. Be cautious if an intermediary claims to control the selection, guarantees an award, or asks for money to “release” a scholarship. Verify any service fee and its scope in writing before agreeing.

Whether you apply for a scholarship or self-fund, compare it with the FEFU tuition and hostel guide and the contract and payment checklist.
