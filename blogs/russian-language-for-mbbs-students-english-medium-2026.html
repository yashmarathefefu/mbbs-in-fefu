Russian Language for MBBS Students in English-Medium Programs

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 6 min read
Program detail: FEFU’s English-track General Medicine documents and the rules for each cohort are the source of truth. This guide is about practical language preparation, not a substitute for the official curriculum.

Students often ask whether an English-medium MBBS means they can ignore Russian. The two questions are different. The language of instruction describes the formal course; Russian helps you navigate life in Vladivostok and communicate in a local environment. Learning the basics early makes the transition easier without changing the program’s published language.

Short answer: FEFU publishes an English-language General Medicine track. Confirm the language and curriculum for your intake, and plan to learn practical Russian alongside your studies.

A Manageable Russian Study Plan

  1. Before travel, learn Cyrillic, numbers, greetings and phrases for emergencies.
  2. Keep a small vocabulary list for food, transport, health and campus directions.
  3. Practise listening every day; pronunciation and comprehension take time.
  4. Ask FEFU about Russian-language classes and whether they are included in your course plan.
  5. For clinical communication, rely on supervised instruction rather than translation apps alone.

Do not let an agent’s phrase “100% English” answer every question. Ask for the official curriculum, cohort-specific timetable, clinical-language expectations and examination language. This matters for both learning and future medical registration in India. Keep course language evidence with your enrollment documents.

English Instruction and NMC Compliance

For an Indian graduate seeking registration, the current Foreign Medical Graduate framework includes requirements that should be checked before admission. NMC notices emphasize the need to verify the program’s medium, duration, curriculum and training. A general university marketing statement is not enough; ask for the course documents applying to your batch.
