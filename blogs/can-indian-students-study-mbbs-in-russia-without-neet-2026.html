Can Indian Students Study MBBS in Russia Without NEET? 2026 Guide

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 6 min read
Independent status: This is a consultancy guide, not an official FEFU or NMC notice. Rules can change; confirm your own eligibility with the current official documents before accepting an offer.

If you searched “MBBS in Russia without NEET,” you are probably trying to understand whether a missed cutoff closes every route. There are two separate decisions: whether a university will consider your application and whether you remain eligible for the Indian medical-registration route you want after graduation.

Short answer: Never treat “direct admission” as proof that NEET is irrelevant. A university sets its admission criteria, while Indian authorities set the conditions for Indian students seeking to practise in India. Check both before paying.

What Indian Students Should Check About NEET

For an Indian student planning to return and practise, the NEET question belongs in the eligibility check before enrolment. Do not rely on a social-media post that says either “NEET is never needed abroad” or “any NEET score is enough.” The applicable rule depends on the current regulations and your admission year.

Keep your NEET result and eligibility records. Then compare them with the current NMC guidance and FEFU’s official admission requirements. If you did not take the exam, did not qualify, or are unsure whether your result remains valid, ask the regulator or a qualified adviser for a written answer tied to your situation.

How to Review FEFU Without Rushing

  1. Ask FEFU for the current rules for the English-track General Medicine program.
  2. Confirm the entrance subjects, language, course length and intake in an official document.
  3. Check the NMC requirements that apply to your cohort and future goal.
  4. Get the university’s admission and fee documents before arranging payment.
  5. Keep the consultancy service contract separate from FEFU’s education contract.

A FEFU-focused adviser can help organize these questions and track documents. A responsible adviser should explain what is confirmed and what still needs an official answer; no counselor can promise admission, a visa, or a future FMGE/NExT result.
