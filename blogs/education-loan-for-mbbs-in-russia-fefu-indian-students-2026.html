Education Loan for MBBS in Russia at FEFU: Indian Student Guide

MBBS Abroads Editorial Team September 24, 2026 7 min read
Loan decisions are lender-specific: This guide is not a loan offer or financial advice. Confirm eligibility, interest, security and repayment terms directly with the lender and current government scheme documents.

Families considering FEFU General Medicine often ask whether an Indian education loan can cover study in Russia. The honest answer is that each lender sets its own rules for overseas courses. Approval, eligible expenses, collateral and repayment terms are not universal.

Check this first: Do not assume that a central government education-loan scheme for eligible institutions in India automatically covers a foreign university. Ask the bank which policy applies to FEFU and get the answer in writing.

PM-Vidyalaxmi: Verify the Eligible Institution List

The Ministry of Education’s PM-Vidyalaxmi scheme guidelines describe a loan product for students admitted to quality higher education institutions in India that meet the scheme’s criteria. Do not assume that an overseas university is eligible under this scheme. Check the latest guidelines and institution list, then ask your bank about separate study-abroad loan products.

Prepare a Loan File Before a Deadline

Ask FEFU for the official offer, annual tuition schedule, payment deadlines and invoice process. Prepare academic records, identity documents, family income or co-borrower papers requested by the lender, and a full-course cost estimate. Keep the fee quote in its original currency and ask the bank how much rupee funding will be required after exchange and transfer charges.

Speak to banks early, but do not borrow against a verbal fee estimate. Compare interest, processing charges, collateral rules, drawdown timing and prepayment terms. The lowest advertised interest rate may not mean the lowest total borrowing cost.

Ask FEFU for current written costs, then use our fees guide and contract verification checklist to organize the information before speaking with a lender.
