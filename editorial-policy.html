Transparency

Editorial policy and information standards

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Important independence disclosure: MBBS Abroads is an education-admissions consultancy and is not Far Eastern Federal University, the National Medical Commission, or a government authority. University and regulatory requirements must be confirmed with the relevant official body before a student pays or travels.

Who publishes our guides

Articles are published by the MBBS Abroads Editorial Team. We use an organizational byline because no individual credentialed author has yet been supplied for publication. We do not attach invented names, qualifications, student identities, or testimonials to our content.

How information is checked

We prioritize primary sources: official FEFU pages and documents, NMC regulations and notices, government visa or migration information, and written university contracts or invoices. Third-party pages may help identify a question, but they are not treated as final proof of a fee, deadline, eligibility rule, recognition status, or licensing outcome.

  • Fees are stated in their source currency where possible; INR conversions are estimates and can change.
  • Admission deadlines and document lists are intake-specific and should be reconfirmed.
  • Safety, accommodation, and student-life claims are qualified when room, building, season, or individual experience can vary.
  • Exam-performance claims require a traceable public source and should not be treated as a guaranteed personal outcome.

Updates and corrections

Publication and modification dates are changed only when a page receives a meaningful factual or editorial update. If you find an error, send the page URL and supporting source through the contact form. Material corrections should be made promptly and reflected in the page's visible review information.

Use of drafting tools

Software and AI-assisted tools may be used for research organization, drafting, formatting, or quality checks. They are not accepted as factual sources. Claims still need human review against primary material before publication.

Commercial disclosure

The site can invite readers to request counseling or admissions support. That commercial purpose does not turn consultancy statements into official university statements. Service fees, university fees, hostel charges, insurance, visa costs, travel costs, and government charges should remain clearly separated in written documents.

What our content cannot promise

Our guides are educational information, not medical, legal, immigration, or regulatory advice. Admission, visas, accommodation, degree recognition, licensing eligibility, and examination results cannot be guaranteed by an article or counselor.
