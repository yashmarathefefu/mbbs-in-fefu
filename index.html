Housing costs

FEFU rates · checked 16 October 2026

Published double-room hostel fees

FEFU lists these charges per bed, per month, in Russian rubles:

  • Buildings 1.8 and 2.1–2.6 · 18–20 m²: RUB 3,100 (RUB 3,200 with a mini-fridge)
  • Buildings 6–11 · 25–32 m²: RUB 3,500–4,100 (RUB 3,600–4,200 with a mini-fridge)

These are FEFU accommodation charges, separate from tuition and the consultancy fee. FEFU confirms the available room and amount for your case. Check FEFU’s official double-room rates.
