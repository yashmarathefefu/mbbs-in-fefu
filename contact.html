Keep the fees clear

Ask for the agreed consultancy scope and one consultancy fee in writing. FEFU tuition and accommodation, travel, visa-related, insurance, and other provider charges are separate and should be identified by the provider. FEFU controls admission and room assignment and confirms the current housing charge; visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities.

Check current details directly with FEFU Admissions and FEFU’s double-room accommodation schedule.
